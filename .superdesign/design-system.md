# WishTwo design direction

Chinese, mobile-first two-person wish list centered on thoughtful gifting. Preserve gift discovery, private personal wishes, savings tracking, completion/archive, and joint experiences. Current app is a local browser demo, not authenticated or synchronized.

Use warm ivory, sage greens and restrained warm accents; legible Chinese body text, serif display headings, generous whitespace. Prioritize the partner wishlist and budget filters over decorative content. Keep one clear add-wish action; preserve mobile bottom navigation. Avoid social-feed features, purchase pressure, invented authentication or gift reservation. Assets: local SVG illustrations in public/art.
