# WishTwo — warm illustrated wish notebook

## Product and behavior
Chinese mobile-first two-person wishlist centered on thoughtful gifting. Home focuses on the partner's wishes, a clear add-my-wish action, favorite status, budget filter and readable reference prices. Keep own wishes, private savings records, completion/archive and joint plans accessible. Current browser-local prototype has no authentication or synchronization; label it 本地演示 · 尚未同步. No gift reservation, feed, chat, payments or invented features.

## Approved new visual direction
The user provided four reference illustrations and asked for a warm girlish illustrated atmosphere. Extract their general visual characteristics only: generous paper whitespace, slightly irregular dark ink contours, softly filled flat shapes, dusty blush cheeks, pale blue flowers, tender grassy greens, butter yellow, tiny cherry-red bows, a playful storybook mood and a crayon-like grain. Create original compositions and characters; do not copy the supplied girl, elephant, mushroom character or signed artworks, and do not reproduce signatures. The references are visual inspiration, not website assets.

## Tokens
Paper #fffdf7; surface #fff8ee; ink #3c342f; muted #887b70; faint outline #e8ded1; dusty rose #e9bdc6; rose hover #d99fab; sage #bdd0a6; pale blue #c9dfe7; butter yellow #f2e3a2; cherry accent #b84940 used sparingly. Buttons rose with dark ink text for legibility. White/cream wish cards with delicate irregular borders and softly tinted illustration panels. Do not use neon colors, dark panels, loud gradients, glass effects or heavy shadows.

## Typography and rhythm
Keep Noto Sans SC for readable Chinese body text, Noto Serif SC for expressive headings, available system fallbacks. Heading 28–34px desktop / 24–28px mobile; body 14–16px; card title 17–18px; meta 12–13px. Handwritten feeling belongs in doodles, labels and composition; do not use illegible handwriting for body text. Spacing 8/12/16/20/24/32/48px; corners 8–16px with a subtly organic feel; tap targets at least 44px. Plenty of blank paper, sparse flowers and bows, low-contrast paper grain behind content only.

## Composition
A keepsake notebook / small gift atelier, not a business dashboard and not a decorative landing page. A modest original vignette of two friendly woodland animals passing a ribbon-wrapped present may accompany the concise heading, with flowers and a small envelope. Partner wishes remain immediately reachable beneath the hero. Cards resemble collectible illustrated notes; category, name, price and favorite badge remain readable. Preserve exact heart brand identity and product name 心愿之间 / WISHTWO. Navigation may be restyled to the new palette; desktop sidebar and mobile bottom navigation remain. Keep real wish content and functions. Prefer original hand-drawn SVG doodles, not emoji confetti.

## Assets and motion
Existing product SVGs can stay in wish cards, or be faithfully rendered in softer colors without changing what each wish depicts. Use only intentional data URLs or public HTTPS assets inside the remote draft. No original reference artworks as page backgrounds or product imagery. Gentle hover motion only and honor prefers-reduced-motion.

## Source CSS contract
The new tokens above intentionally supersede the old sage-only palette in style.css. Source files describe existing structure and behavior, not the desired old visual styling. Apply this new direction across sidebar, header, hero, cards, filters and mobile navigation consistently.
